Fast Loop: End-to-End Self-Improving Robotics Training

Can robots teach themselves to get better — recursively? We built Fast Loop, an end-to-end self-improving training loop for robotics using SmolVLA + LoRA fine-tuning, with Claude Opus as the expert AI teacher. The Problem

Training robots has traditionally been a slow, time-consuming bottleneck. Every improvement requires human expertise, labeled data, and days of retraining. We asked a fundamental question: Can we achieve recursive self-improvement in robotics?

Fast Loop answers yes — by letting the robot learn from an AI teacher (Claude Opus), evaluate its own policy quality, and fine-tune itself in a closed loop — no human in the loop required.

Architecture — The Self-Improving Loop

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │ Fast Loop · Self-Improving Cycle │ └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘ Antioch 3D Scene Editor (motion profiles, simulation environment) │ ▼ SmolVLA Policy Execution (robot arm performs manipulation task) │ ▼ Claude Opus — Expert Teacher (evaluates trajectory quality, generates feedback, produces improved demonstration labels) │ ▼ LoRA Fine-Tuning (lightweight adaptation of SmolVLA on teacher labels) │ ▼ Improved Policy ──────────────────────▶ ↻ Iterate
